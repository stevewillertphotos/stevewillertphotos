Aurora Borealis
Le Dun, Quebec

Quebec, Canada  ·  Northern Sky

We almost missed it. After a cloudy week and a failed sunset shoot, a NOAA app check at dinner changed everything. We rushed back to the cabin, slid open the kitchen door a few times to check the sky, and finally snapped a test shot that showed a faint green glow. That was all we needed. The lights came out and didn't stop for hours.

The Photographer

About Steve

I'm a photography hobbyist who loves to travel and shoot landscapes. Most of my best photos happen on trips with friends — we plan around chasing good light, but honestly some of the best moments are the ones we stumble into.

The aurora shots are a good example. It was a rainy week, nothing was going as planned, and then a single notification on a weather app turned the whole trip around. That's kind of the thing I love most about it.
